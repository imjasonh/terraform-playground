# Fixture provenance

These are the Wolfenstein 3D **shareware** v1.0 episode data files
(`*.WL1`). The shareware episode was released by id Software/Apogee for
free distribution ("you are encouraged to share this episode"), and these
exact files are widely redistributed (mirrored here from the
`ccie18473/wolf4rust` repository, which vendors the shareware episode).

They are used only as golden-test fixtures for the format codecs and as an
optional demo dataset for the editor. The registered episodes (`*.WL6`) and
Spear of Destiny data are NOT included and must be supplied by the user.
